The market today

A busy, seasonal coastal market

About 350 short-stay homes with two to four bedrooms compete around Apollo Bay. Over the past twelve months they were booked about half of all nights, and nightly rates rose.

  • Rates are rising. The area's average nightly rate was A$441 over the past year, up 4.9% on the year before.
  • Summer leads the year. Market occupancy reached 74% in January and 65% in December, and still averaged about 40% through winter.
  • Visitors keep coming. Victoria's visitor spending hit a record A$48.6 billion, and the Great Ocean Road was voted Australia's best road trip in the 2026 Australian Traveller Readers' Choice Awards.

How guests book

Late bookings and short breaks

Guests book closer to arrival than they used to, and most stays are two to four nights. A home earns the most when its calendar and minimum stays are managed day by day.
