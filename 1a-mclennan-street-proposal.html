Where we expect The Ashman House to sit

Low to Standard in the first year, around net to you, moving to Standard or better from year two. Family groups who want to walk to the beach are what Apollo Bay runs short of in the holidays, and homes with room for them book early for summer, Easter and the school breaks. The first year is held back only by the things every new listing has to earn: reviews, a search ranking and repeat guests. Comparable homes have been running 43–49% booked over the past three months, well above either scenario, so the upside is real; we'd rather you be pleasantly surprised than promised something we can't control.

How we look after the home and your guests

In-house cleaning

$300per clean · charged to you after each guest departs

Our own employed team, not contractors, with a property check after every stay. Two cleaners, about two hours on site, plus all guest supplies: bathroom products, tea and coffee, milk, toilet paper, bin liners, and a bottle of local wine with a handwritten note. Guests pay a flat $100 cleaning fee towards this; the balance is built into your nightly rate.

What's included, and what isn't

Every clean: vacuum and mop throughout; bathrooms, showers and toilets; kitchen and all surfaces; beds made; a cosmetic sweep and tidy of decks, courtyards and the barbecue area; bins out or in; a photo of every room and a property check before the next arrival. Over time we also get to the under-furniture, skirting and cupboard tidying that keeps a home feeling cared for.

Supplies we stock for guests, included in the price: toilet paper, bin liners, dishwashing tablets and detergent, cloths and sponges, spray and wipe, laundry powder, hand wash, body wash, shampoo and conditioner, tea, coffee sachets, sugar, long-life milk, salt, pepper, olive oil, paper towel, cling wrap and foil, fly spray, matches and firelighters where there's a fire, and the arrival gift: a bottle of local wine and a handwritten note. We use Who Gives a Crap paper and Biology bathroom products.

Not part of a regular clean (we quote these as a deep clean once or twice a year, or you do them on your own visits): inside and outside windows, external cobwebs, pressure-washing decks, sugar-soaping walls, steam-cleaning grout, carpets and upholstery, heavy oven and barbecue cleaning, gutters, moving fridges and beds to clean behind, exhaust fans and air-conditioner filters, and dry-cleaning quilts.

You supply: firewood and kindling stock, coffee pods or beans, boxed tissues, light globes and remote batteries, gas bottles, pegs, hairdryers, vacuum bags and mop heads, barbecue cleaning products, spices and sauces, and any pantry extras you'd like guests to have. A locked owner's cupboard with spares of these means we can restock between guests without waiting on a delivery.

Linen

What's included, and what isn't

Included, every booking: beds made before arrival with hotel-quality white linen: fitted and flat sheets, pillowcases and white quilt covers for every bed (bunk linen is supplied folded for guests to make up, as our staff don't make bunks for safety reasons), bath towels and face washers for every guest, hand towels and bath mats for every bathroom, and tea towels for the kitchen. All commercially laundered between stays.

You supply once, at set-up: quilts (all-season warmth), two pillows per sleeping space (a firm and a softer is ideal), mattress and pillow protectors for every bed plus spares, and a spare warm blanket per bed. We'll tell you exactly what's needed on the set-up visit.

Not included: owner linen; we don't launder your own sheets or towels. Professional cleaning of quilts, mattress protectors or bulky items that won't fit a domestic machine is arranged as needed at about $10 per protector and up to $30 per quilt. Beach towels, pool towels and robes aren't part of the standard package but can be added.

Your own stays: you arrive to made beds with our linen and the $170 applies; if you'd rather bring your own, say so on the owner booking form and it's waived.

    Working with Otway Retreats
    
What it's like, week to week

    
How we look after your home and your guests once we're working together. Our styling guide and inventory checklist come with the management authority.

  

  

    
Getting the home ready
We walk the house with you and advise on presentation, amenities and house rules, then prepare a condition report and photographed inventory as the baseline. We arrange the professional shoot, write the listing copy, set the home up on Airbnb, Booking.com, Google and our own site, and build a digital guest guide specific to your home: appliances, heating, WiFi, bins, house rules, checkout, and the best of the region.

    
Every changeover
Every one of our cleaners is an Otway Retreats employee, not a contractor. That is one of the biggest differences between us and other operators: the same trained people look after your home every time, they know it, and they care how it is handed over. They clean to a checklist built for your home, restock the supplies and make the beds with hired hotel-grade linen.
You see the result after every clean. Our team photographs every room and completes a detailed cleaning report on each visit. Wear, damage or anything missing is picked up within 72 hours of a departure and recorded against those photos, so you always have a dated record of how your home was presented. Where a guest is at fault, we claim against their bond on your behalf.

    
A property check before every arrival
After the clean, and before any guest walks in, a member of our senior management team checks the home. It is a second set of eyes on top of the cleaning report: anything small the cleaners have missed is actioned on the spot, and dust, bugs, a flickering globe or a bin that needs emptying are caught before a guest can arrive to them. The check also keeps our own standards honest: issues are picked up quickly, fed back into training, and the home is handed over guest-ready every single time.
Reviews are everything in this market. This step is one of the main reasons our homes hold five-star ratings and Otway Retreats holds Superhost status, and that reputation is what keeps a listing near the top of the search results and booking.

    
Guests, around the clock
Guests can reach us at any hour. We handle check-in questions and troubleshoot problems before calling a trade. Our concierge arranges transport, tours, hampers and platters, private dining, in-home yoga and personal chefs. It is one of the most valued parts of our service and a large reason our homes collect five-star reviews and repeat stays.

    
Maintenance and the house itself
An in-house maintenance contractor handles the small things between bookings; qualified, insured local trades handle the rest, always with your approval unless it's an emergency. We monitor gas bottles and firewood, manage bins, arrange lawns and gardens through local partners, and each year send you an inspection report listing the deep-clean and upkeep items worth doing: high windows, carpets, decks, grout.

    
Pricing, actively managed
What it is. Your nightly rate is never a single fixed number. We agree a floor, a base and a ceiling for each season with you, and PriceLabs, the dynamic pricing system we run across every home we manage, moves the rate inside those limits every night. It reads how booked Apollo Bay is for each date, what comparable homes are charging, how far out the date is, the day of the week and local events, and reprices your calendar accordingly, in the same way airlines and hotels do.
Why it earns you more. A fixed rate is wrong most of the year: too high when the town is quiet, so the home sits empty, and too low on the dates everyone wants, so you leave money behind. Dynamic pricing lifts the rate when demand is strong (summer, Easter, long weekends, event weekends) and eases it when a date is still open close to arrival, which fills nights that would otherwise earn nothing. Across our portfolio that means higher occupancy in the shoulder months and higher rates in peak, which is where most of a year's income comes from. We review the limits with you as the listing builds a history, and the guest experience stays central because reviews and perceived value drive your search ranking as much as price does.

    
Your bookings, in your pocket
You're set up with your own login to our booking system from day one. Every time a guest books you get an alert, and the app shows the live calendar: who is arriving, when, for how many nights, and what's coming up. You can see the whole year at a glance, so you're never wondering whether the house is busy. The login is view-only; nightly pricing is managed on our side.

    
Paid weekly, every Thursday
Most managers in the area pay owners monthly. We pay every week. Booking income lands in your account each Thursday, less management, cleaning, linen and any approved invoices we have paid for you, so your money isn't sitting in someone else's account for weeks after a guest has stayed. You receive itemised statements with the invoices attached and an owner portal to look back through them. Each July we generate your end-of-financial-year report: every dollar of income and every expense for the year in one document, ready to hand straight to your accountant. We can pay repairs, replacements and gas on your behalf so everything sits on one statement, and we'll point you to a tax depreciation schedule if you don't already have one.

    
Protecting you
The one thing we must have in place before we manage the home is public liability cover of at least $20 million; it is a legal requirement for us, and it is generally included in a building insurance policy. Beyond that, we recommend you speak with your insurer or broker about building, contents and short-stay rental cover so you're protected for holiday letting (EBM and Terri Scheer both offer suitable policies). Smoke alarms must be compliant, and pools or spas certified where relevant. Every contractor we engage is insured and qualified. For direct bookings, Victoria's short-stay levy is the owner's responsibility to remit; the platforms handle it for Airbnb and Booking.com stays, and we'll point you to the right guidance.

    
Your own stays
When you'd like the home for yourselves, send us the short owner booking form on our website with your dates and preferences and we block the calendar for you. Beds are made with our linen by default; if you'd rather use your own linen, or clean and reset the home yourself afterwards, say so on the form and those charges come off. For Christmas and Easter, let us know before the calendar opens to guests (nine months ahead) so the dates are held.
