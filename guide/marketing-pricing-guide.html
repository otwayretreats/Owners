Marketing

Where your home is seen

Your home is listed on four channels, all fed from one booking system so the calendar and rates stay in step everywhere. A booking on one channel closes those dates on the others within minutes.

AirbnbOur largest source of bookings. Otway Retreats holds Superhost status across the portfolio.
Booking.comReaches international and last-minute travellers along the Great Ocean Road.
GoogleYour home appears in Google's holiday-rental search, linking to our website.
otwayretreats.com.auOur own site. Booking direct is always the cheapest total for guests, and repeat guests come back here.

We don't list on Stayz. Keeping to four well-run channels means fewer double-bookings, consistent pricing and more of the platform's search ranking concentrated on each listing.

Photography

A professional shoot

Fifteen edited images, with the home styled before the shoot. $350 once, waived if you already have high-resolution photos (1920 × 1440 or larger).

Listing copy

Written for each home

We write the title, description and highlights ourselves, naming the real views, beds and walking distances, and keep them matched to what guests find on arrival.

Guest guide

A digital guide per home

Appliances, heating, Wi-Fi, bins, house rules, checkout and the best of the region. Fewer questions, smoother stays and better reviews.

Reviews

Reputation drives ranking

A senior team member checks every home before each arrival. That step is a large part of why our homes hold five-star ratings, and ratings keep a listing near the top of search.

Concierge

Extras that lift reviews

Platters, hampers, grocery delivery, private dining, in-home yoga and transport. Guests remember them and say so in their reviews.

Launch

Seeding a new listing

A new home starts with 20% off its first three Airbnb bookings. Those first reviews earn the listing its search position, and full rates follow.

Annual marketing subscription

Charged at commencement and on each anniversary, at the amount in your management authority ($500 for new agreements in 2026–27). It covers the channel connections, the booking and messaging software, and the PriceLabs dynamic pricing that runs your calendar. All amounts include GST.

Your part

Presentation that earns its rate

Price follows presentation. The homes that hold the top of the market have a few things in common, and most of them are inexpensive.

Style for the photos

We walk the house with you before the shoot and advise on styling, amenities and house rules. Our styling guide and inventory checklist come with the management authority.

Families book in groups

In family homes a cot and high chair are the bare minimum, plus games and books. Target and Kmart are fine for these.

Winter fires sell winter

Homes with a wood fire need firewood for guests from April to November (about 3 m³ over a winter), fireplace tools and a metal ash bucket. We can arrange local deliveries.

Book your own stays early

The calendar opens to guests nine months ahead. Send your Christmas and Easter dates before then and we hold them; after that they go to the market.

Pricing

A rate that moves every night

Your nightly rate is never a single fixed number. We agree a floor, a base and a ceiling for each season with you, and PriceLabs, the dynamic pricing system we run across every home we manage, moves the rate inside those limits every night.

What it reads

Demand, date by date

How booked Apollo Bay is for each night, what comparable homes are charging, how far away the date is, the day of the week and local events such as the Marathon weekend.

Why it earns more

A fixed rate is wrong most of the year

Too high when town is quiet, so the home sits empty; too low on the dates everyone wants. Dynamic pricing lifts the rate when demand is strong and eases it when a date is still open close to arrival.

Where income comes from

Summer, Easter and long weekends

This is a seasonal coastal market. Across our homes, December and January alone brought in 40% of the past year's booking income, so that is where we push rates hardest.

Our position

Above the local median

Over summer our homes typically average around $1,000 a night, well above the local market median. Guests pay for the service: our own cleaners, a property check before every stay and a concierge on call.

When our homes earn their income

Each month's share of booking income across every home we manage, December 2025 to October 2026.

Two out of every three dollars came in between December and April. The winter months still matter: they cover running costs and keep the listing active in search, which is why rates ease to keep them filling.

What guests pay

Channel fees sit on top of your rate

Airbnb and Booking.com charge their own fees. We add those on top of your nightly rate so they never come out of your return. Your statements show what you receive, not the marked-up channel price.

Flat $100 cleaning fee

Every guest pays the same $100 per stay across the portfolio, with nothing else added. It comes back against each clean; the balance of cleaning is built into the nightly rate so totals stay competitive.

Extra guests

Rates are set for the home's standard number of guests. Above that, $40 per person per night.

Pets

$50 per stay, on pet-friendly homes only.

Short-stay levy

Victoria's 7.5% levy is collected by Airbnb and Booking.com on their bookings. On direct bookings it is the owner's to remit, and we point you to the right guidance.

Questions owners ask

Common questions

Why is my rate lower this week than last?

The date is closer and still open, so the system is easing the rate to fill it. A night sold at a lower rate earns more than a night left empty. It will never go below the floor we agreed.

Can I set a fixed rate instead?

You can ask, but we don't recommend it. A fixed rate is too high when town is quiet and too low on the dates everyone wants. We'd rather agree the floor and ceiling with you and review them as the home builds a booking history.

Why doesn't the Airbnb price match my statement?

Airbnb's price includes about 17.1% to cover its own fees, which the guest pays. Your statement shows your rate and what you receive.

Can I block dates myself?

Your login is view-only. Send your dates on the owner booking form on our website and we block them for you, with your preferences for linen and cleaning.

Why aren't you on Stayz?

Four well-run channels give us consistent prices, fewer double-bookings and stronger ranking on each. Booking direct on our own site is where repeat guests return.

My home isn't booking. What happens?

We look at price against the market first, then photos, listing copy and reviews. If a home goes a few weeks without a booking, rates are trimmed 5–15% for the following month, and we'll talk to you about anything bigger.
