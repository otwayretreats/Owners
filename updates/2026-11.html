  • December is softer across the whole area. It's about nine points behind last year. Guests are booking closer to their dates, so we expect December to keep filling over the next few weeks, but rates need to stay sharp.

What we're doing for your home

  • December rates reviewed home by home against live market data, so empty nights are priced to sell and peak nights hold their value.
  • Minimum stays eased where they're holding bookings back, and last-minute pricing on, so gaps fill rather than sit empty.
  • Direct-booking specials, all year round on our website: SUMMERFUN (20% off 7+ nights), RELAX15 (15% off 5+ nights) and SURFSUP10 (10% off 3+ nights). With cost-of-living pressure, guests are taking shorter trips, so these specials are there to encourage longer stays.

Proud sponsor · Apollo Bay

Otway Ocean Flow Festival

Saturday 1 & Sunday 2 May 2027

We're proud to sponsor the first Otway Ocean Flow, a wellness festival set between the Otways rainforest and the Southern Ocean. Expect keynote talks, live music and performance, yoga, ocean plunges, workshops and nature experiences across five venues in Apollo Bay, with more than 50 local practitioners taking part.

  • Visitors in the quiet season. Organisers expect around 1,000 people, in May when town is usually quiet.
  • Built for longer stays. The festival encourages visitors to stay at least two nights, and there are no food trucks on site, so guests eat in town.
  • 7.5% off with code GETINTHEFLOW. It covers stays either side of the festival weekend too, so guests can make a longer break of it.

We'll price the weekend as an event weekend, so if you'd like to use your home then, please let us know early. Early-bird tickets are on sale now at otwayoceanflow.com.au.

Important · Airbnb policy change from 20 November

Guests can no longer be asked to clean

Airbnb's updated house rules mean guests can't be asked to do cleaner-type tasks such as stripping beds or washing dishes. Our listings and cleaning already follow the new rules, so your home stays compliant and well ranked, with nothing you need to do.

New option for summer

Bins over summer: opt in to a bin service

Summer bins fill fast, and under Airbnb's new rules we can no longer ask guests to put bins out. Here's how bins work with us now, and a simple local service if you'd like peace of mind.

What we do

  • Put bins out and bring them in whenever we're at your home for a clean or visit.
  • Most weeks this works well, as long as a clean lines up with bin day.

What we don't do

  • Make special trips just for bins.
  • So if bin day falls between cleans, a collection can be missed or bins left out.

Bin Concierge with Andrew Crouch

Andrew is Apollo Bay's local bin concierge. He'll take your bins out and bring them back on bin day. We give him access to our bookings, so he only comes when guests have stayed, which keeps your costs down. No calls needed.

Around the region

Twelve Apostles

New visitor centre, and bookings now needed

The new Keerray Woorroong Visitor Centre at the Twelve Apostles opened in October. Visitors now need to book online before they go, because a booking is needed to get into the car park. It takes about 30 seconds at twelveapostles.org.au.

  • Free until April 2027. Entry fees are expected from late April 2027. The amounts, including family and concession rates and a locals exemption, haven't been published yet. We'll share them once they are.
  • Better on-site experience. Shuttles for visitors with mobility needs, clearer car park signage, and more local produce in the shop and café.
  • What we're doing: adding the booking link and the fee timing to our guest information, so your guests arrive prepared rather than turned away at the gate.
  • Apollo Bay Harbour: the new harbour seawall is finished. It's built to last 50 years and protects the slipway and the Fishermen's Co-op building from storms and erosion. The wider harbour redevelopment is due in 2027.
  • Wye River to Kennett River trail: the 4.7 km coastal walk has reopened with 678 new stairs and new lookouts over the Southern Ocean. A great tip for guests.

Fire season

Summer checklist

Get the BBQ summer-ready

It's one of the most-used things in your home over summer, and a quick check now makes a real difference to reviews.

  • A second gas bottle on site, so there's always a full spare. When one runs out, we swap it straight away.
  • BBQ tools & cleaning products: tongs, a scraper or brush, and degreaser.
  • BBQ in good condition: burners lighting, hotplate clean, nothing rusted through. We can arrange a service.
  • Outdoor setting spruced up: clean, sturdy furniture and fresh cushions.
