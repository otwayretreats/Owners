Your pricing strategy in PriceLabs

PriceLabs sets a new rate for every night, every day, using three settings we agree with you.

Minimum priceThe floor. PriceLabs never sets a nightly rate below this, even for a quiet night that hasn't booked close to the date. It protects the value of your home; set too high, quiet nights are more likely to stay empty. Booking site promotions are applied after this, so a discounted booking can pay out a little less per night.
$330
Base priceThe anchor. It is the rate for a typical night across the year. PriceLabs moves each night up or down from it for the season, weekends and weekdays, local events and demand, and how quickly your calendar is filling. Guests rarely see the base price itself; raising or lowering it shifts every night.
$490
Maximum priceThe ceiling. PriceLabs never sets a rate above this, even on the busiest nights such as New Year's Eve. If peak nights keep reaching the maximum and still book, there may be room to lift it.
$900

From 23 December your nights sit at your $900 maximum; PriceLabs would price some higher, but with none of them booked yet we aren't suggesting a change. PriceLabs currently suggests a base price of $422 against your $490.
