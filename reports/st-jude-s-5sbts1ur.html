Minimum price, $395. PriceLabs never sets a nightly rate below this, even for a quiet night that hasn't booked close to the date. It protects the value of your home; set too high, quiet nights are more likely to stay empty. Booking site promotions are applied after this, so a discounted booking can pay out a little less per night.

Base price, $525. PriceLabs moves each night up or down from the base for the season, weekends and weekdays, local events and demand, and how quickly your calendar is filling. Guests rarely see the base price itself; raising or lowering it shifts every night.

Maximum price, $1,000. PriceLabs never sets a rate above this, even on the busiest nights such as New Year's Eve. If peak nights keep reaching the maximum and still book, there may be room to lift it.

Most open weeknights from now to early December are already at your $395 a night minimum, so PriceLabs has eased them as far as your settings allow.
